Article 12 - Work at height

1. The employer shall organise work so that tasks which can be carried out
from the ground are not carried out at height.

2. Where work at height cannot be avoided, collective protective equipment,
in particular guardrails and safety nets, shall be given priority over
personal fall-arrest equipment.

3. Guardrails shall be at least 1.00 metre high, fitted with an intermediate
rail and with a toe board of at least 0.15 metre.

4. Personal fall-arrest equipment shall be inspected by a competent person at
least once every twelve months. Each inspection shall be recorded in writing.

5. Work at height shall not be carried out by a worker working alone. A second
worker shall be present for the duration of the work.

6. Workers shall receive training before carrying out work at height for the
first time. This training shall be renewed at least every three years.

7. A written work permit shall be issued before any work carried out at a
height exceeding three metres.

8. Work at height shall be suspended where the wind speed exceeds 50 km/h.

9. Before work begins, a rescue plan shall be established allowing a worker
suspended in a harness to be recovered within fifteen minutes.

10. Ladders and stepladders may be used as a workstation only for
short-duration light work.
